The Story

A garden table for
everyday cravings

Tucked into a quiet Jaihindpuram lane, The Munchies has built its name on a simple promise: purely vegetarian food, cooked fresh, served in an open-air setting that's as easy for a birthday of fifty as it is for two people splitting a pizza on a Tuesday night.

Wood-fired pizza oven
Chinese, Continental & Indian menu
Open-air seating, ~50 guests
Clean, family-friendly space
